## Important concepts

### Deception as detection

A honeypot has no legitimate production purpose, so any interaction is suspicious. That makes it a high-signal detection source and a way to study attacker behaviour without exposing real assets.

### Containment is essential

A compromised honeypot must not become a pivot into the real network. Isolation, logging, egress filtering and clear rules of engagement are prerequisites, not optional hardening.

## Practical notes

The chapter demonstrates a basic honeypot in a lab. Keep any honeypot on an isolated segment with no route to production systems.

<div class="notice notice-warning"><p><strong>Safety and legality:</strong> Do not deploy a honeypot on a network you do not control. Enticing or recording real users can create legal and privacy obligations, and a poorly contained honeypot is a liability.</p></div>

## FAQ

### Is running a honeypot legal?

Generally yes on your own network, but recording attackers and handling their data creates legal and privacy duties that vary by jurisdiction.

### What is the difference between low- and high-interaction honeypots?

Low-interaction honeypots emulate limited services and are safer and easier to run. High-interaction honeypots provide real systems, yielding richer data but much greater risk.
