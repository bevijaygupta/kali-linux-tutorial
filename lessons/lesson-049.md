## Important concepts

### Online versus offline

An online attack guesses against a live service and is limited by network latency, rate limits and account lockouts. An offline attack guesses against captured hashes at local hardware speed, which is why a stolen hash database is far more dangerous than a login form.

### Why passwords still matter

Passwords remain the most common authentication factor. Reuse, weak patterns and breached credentials make them valuable even when systems use modern hashing, because attackers can try known passwords against many accounts.

## FAQ

### Which is faster: online or offline cracking?

Offline cracking is usually much faster because there is no network round trip, rate limit or lockout. Speed depends on hash type and hardware.

### What is credential stuffing?

Trying username and password pairs leaked from one breach against other services, exploiting password reuse.
