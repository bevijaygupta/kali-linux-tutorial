## Important concepts

### Kali is not an anonymity system

Kali is a distribution full of security tools. It does not change how your traffic is routed. Unless you deliberately use Tor, a VPN or another proxy, your requests leave your network with your normal public IP address and your DNS queries follow your system configuration.

### Anonymity, privacy, security

Security protects data from unauthorized access. Privacy controls who can collect and share information about you. Anonymity hides who you are. A VPN can improve privacy on a local network without making you anonymous to the VPN provider, which is why the three terms should not be used interchangeably.

## FAQ

### Does Kali Linux hide my IP address automatically?

No. Kali does not route your traffic through Tor or a VPN unless you configure it to. Your public IP is visible to destinations by default.

### Is a VPN the same as anonymity?

No. A VPN hides your IP from the destination but the provider can still see your traffic and identity. It is a privacy tool, not a guarantee of anonymity.
