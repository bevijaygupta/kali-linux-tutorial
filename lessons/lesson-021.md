## Important concepts

### Forward and reverse proxies

A forward proxy sits in front of clients and forwards their requests outward; it is what people usually mean by a proxy. A reverse proxy sits in front of servers, accepts requests on their behalf and is common for load balancing, caching and TLS termination.

### A proxy is not anonymity by itself

The destination sees the proxy's address, but the proxy operator sees your address and traffic metadata. Logs, browser fingerprints and unencrypted content can still identify you. Chaining proxies adds complexity without guaranteeing privacy.

## FAQ

### Does a proxy hide my IP address from websites?

It hides your address from the destination, which sees the proxy instead. The proxy itself still knows your address.

### What is the difference between HTTP and SOCKS proxies?

HTTP proxies are designed for web traffic and understand HTTP semantics. SOCKS proxies forward arbitrary TCP connections, so they can carry more protocols.
