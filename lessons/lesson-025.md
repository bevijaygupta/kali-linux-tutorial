## Important concepts

### Three layers of the web

The surface web is indexed by search engines. The deep web is content not indexed, such as private accounts and databases. The dark web is a small set of services reachable only through anonymity networks such as Tor. Using Tor is not itself illegal in most places; the legality of what you do remains unchanged.

### Onion routing in one sentence

Tor chooses a path of relays and encrypts traffic in layers so each relay knows only its predecessor and successor. No single relay sees both who you are and where you are going, which is the property the design is built around.

## FAQ

### Is using Tor illegal?

In most countries, using Tor is legal. Laws target criminal activity, not the network itself. Check your local regulations if you are unsure.

### Can I be tracked on Tor?

Tor resists network-level tracking, but logins, browser fingerprints, downloaded files and misconfigured applications can still reveal your identity.
