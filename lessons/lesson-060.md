## Important concepts

### Capture and impersonation

Sniffing observes packets; spoofing changes how a device or identity appears so traffic is sent somewhere else. They are often combined: spoofing redirects traffic, then sniffing captures it.

### Switches changed the game

Modern switched networks do not broadcast every frame to every port, which limits passive capture. Techniques such as ARP spoofing or port mirroring are needed to see other devices' traffic, and both require authorization.

## FAQ

### Can I sniff traffic on a switched network?

Not all of it by default. You see broadcast traffic and your own unicast traffic unless you use an authorized technique such as port mirroring or ARP spoofing.

### Does HTTPS stop sniffing?

It stops content from being read in plaintext, but metadata such as destination addresses and timing may still be visible.
