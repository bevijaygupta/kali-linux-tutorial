## Important concepts

### The encrypted tunnel

A VPN client creates an encrypted connection to a VPN server. Traffic inside the tunnel is protected from the local network and the ISP, then exits to the internet from the VPN server. The destination sees the server's address, while the VPN provider can associate activity with your account.

### Protocol choices

OpenVPN is mature and widely supported, WireGuard is modern and lightweight, and IPsec is common in enterprise site-to-site deployments. Protocol choice affects performance, compatibility and configuration complexity more than it affects the basic privacy model.

## FAQ

### Does a VPN make me anonymous?

No. It hides your IP from destinations, but the VPN provider can still link traffic to your account, and browser or login identifiers can reveal you.

### VPN or Tor: which is better?

They solve different problems. A VPN is simpler and faster for privacy from local networks; Tor is designed to resist traffic correlation but is slower and not a substitute for safe behaviour.
