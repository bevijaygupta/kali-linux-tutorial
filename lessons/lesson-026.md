## Important concepts

### From localhost to .onion

A hidden service tells the Tor daemon to forward connections arriving at a .onion address to a local port, such as the Apache server on 127.0.0.1:80. The service's private key determines the address, so losing or exposing the key compromises the identity of the service.

### Operational hygiene

A test page should contain no personal details, analytics, external fonts or images that reveal your clearnet infrastructure. Hidden services are a privacy technology, but they are only as private as the server configuration around them.

## Practical notes

The lab uses XAMPP and Tor on your own VM. The exact Tor configuration is demonstrated step by step in the video.

| --- | --- |

## FAQ

### Do I need a public IP to host a .onion site?

No. Tor introduces circuits to your local service, so the machine does not need an inbound public address.

### Why does my .onion address change?

The address is derived from the service's key. Regenerating the key produces a new address, so back up the original if you need it to stay stable.
