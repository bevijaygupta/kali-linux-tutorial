## Important concepts

### Wireless is a shared medium

Unlike a switched wired network, radio frames are broadcast through the air. Encryption protects content, but management frames and metadata are still visible to nearby adapters. That is why wireless assessment starts with understanding frames and encryption.

### Handshake capture is not cracking

WPA2 cracking usually requires a captured four-way handshake or PMKID, then an offline dictionary attack against it. A strong, unique passphrase makes that attack impractical. WEP is different: its design flaws allow key recovery from collected traffic.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between SSID and BSSID?

The SSID is the network name users see. The BSSID is the MAC address of a specific access point radio.

### Is WEP still used?

Rarely, and it should not be. WEP is fundamentally broken and can be cracked quickly regardless of password strength.
