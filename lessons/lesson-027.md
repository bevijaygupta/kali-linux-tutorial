## Important concepts

### Who sees what

The entry relay knows your network address but not the destination. The middle relay knows neither end. The exit relay knows the destination and the unencrypted traffic if the site uses plain HTTP. End-to-end HTTPS protects content, while Tor protects the relationship between source and destination.

### Limits remain

Tor does not protect you from logging into an identified account, downloading and opening documents that contact the network directly, or a compromised endpoint. Global adversaries who observe large parts of the network may also attempt traffic correlation.

## FAQ

### Can the exit node see my traffic?

It can see traffic to the destination if that traffic is unencrypted. HTTPS prevents the exit node from reading content, though it still sees the destination address.

### Does Tor hide the fact that I am using Tor?

No. Your ISP or network operator can usually tell that you are connecting to Tor, even though they cannot see your destinations.
