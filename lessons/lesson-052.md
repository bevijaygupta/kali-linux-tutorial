## Important concepts

### The pipeline

Obtain a hash, identify its format, choose a tool and wordlist, run the attack, then verify and record the result. Each stage is a source of failure, so changing one variable at a time makes troubleshooting tractable.

### Success is probabilistic

A wordlist attack succeeds only if the password is in the list or can be derived by a rule. Large wordlists cost time and storage, and slow hashes make GPU time the limiting factor.

## FAQ

### Why did my hash not crack?

The password may not be in the wordlist, the hash type may be wrong, or the file format may not match what the tool expects.

### Does a bigger wordlist always help?

It increases coverage but also runtime. Targeted lists and rules often outperform a huge generic list.
