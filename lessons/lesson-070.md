## Important concepts

### A front end, not a different attack

Fern orchestrates the same underlying tools and techniques. Learning the command-line process first makes the GUI understandable and helps when a step fails silently.

### Automation hides assumptions

GUI tools choose interfaces, channels and attack steps for you. That convenience can obscure what is happening and make it easier to act outside your authorization. Verify the target and scope before starting any automated attack.

## FAQ

### Is Fern better than Aircrack-ng?

It is easier for beginners because it automates steps. The command line gives more control and better diagnostics.

### Why does Fern not detect my adapter?

The driver may not support monitor mode or injection, or the interface may be in use. Check adapter compatibility first.
